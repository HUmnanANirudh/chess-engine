KEY ALGORITHMS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Minimax with Alpha-Beta Pruning:
  • Evaluates all possible moves and counter-moves
  • Prunes branches that can't improve the result
  • 50-90% faster than pure Minimax
  • Depth 5 for gameplay (1-2 seconds per move)
  • Depth 7 for analysis (5-10 seconds per game)

Position Evaluation:
  • Material value (pawn=1, knight=3, bishop=3, rook=5, queen=9)
  • Piece-square tables (positional bonuses)
  • Combined score for accurate evaluation

Move Probability Analysis:
  • Evaluates all legal moves at depth 7
  • Identifies top moves (within 50 centipawns of best)
  • Calculates probability: (1 / |top_moves|) × 100%
  • Shows how correct each human move was
